Build your story graph
  1. Bring in references
    Mirror Storage → open a profile → add the whole profile or selected posts. A container references its dataset; it does not download it again.
  2. Arrange your board
    Drag a card header to move its owned branch, or drag empty card space to move that card. Drag its corner to resize. Wheel over the graph to zoom around your pointer; drag empty canvas to pan. Overview fits the board.
  3. Draw a connection
    Drag a connector circle onto another node’s connector to create a wire. Or click one port, then the other. Name the relationship and choose From → To, reverse, or both directions. Save to keep it. Escape cancels the first-port selection.
  4. Merge references
    Add a Junction, connect reference nodes to it, then connect it to interpretation/output. Add a Music node and connect it to the appropriate junction.
  5. Review and edit
    Click a wire or use Connect nodes → Existing connection. Choose curved, straight, or right-angle wires; change direction or delete the connection. Edit wire bends shows draggable handles. Right-click a node for precise placement and deletion. Simulate flow previews enabled wires; it does not process data.
